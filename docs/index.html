Documentation

All of it, in one place.

Five guides, grouped by what each one sets up. They are independent, so start wherever you like: give her a mind, add a voice if you want her to sound like somebody in particular, and teach her to do things whenever you feel like it.

5 guides Mind · voice · agents Start anywhere

Setup the Mind

How to set up the AI model. She needs one of these to think — pick whichever suits you. They do not combine: one provider, one set of keys.

Hailo-Ollama →

With an AI HAT+ 2 attached, the model runs on the NPU entirely on the Pi. Nothing leaves the machine and nothing costs per token.

100% local No API key AI HAT+ 2

Gemini →

The default and the easiest to start with: a free key, no account to manage, no billing. Five minutes and she starts answering.

Free tier No card needed ~5 minutes

Bedrock →

Run her on Claude. It costs real money per token, but no key is ever written into her config and the whole setup is five commands.

Claude Pay per token ~15 minutes

Setup the Voice

How to set up text to speech. Separately from the mind: every reply she writes is also spoken.

Typecast →

A Typecast account, an API key and a voice id, so every reply is spoken. You can audition voices on their site before you spend a character of your quota.

Works out of the box Free tier available ~5 minutes
You can skip this one

A demo key and voice ship with the app, so she talks from the first launch. Setting up your own account is about getting your voice and not sharing a rate limit with everybody else on the free tier.

Agents

About agents — what they are and how to control them. Not setup, exactly, but the same family of job.

Agents →

Drop a folder in and she can do new things: play your music, control the pet, or run a script you wrote. Any language, no rebuild, and no second tool — you install, list and delete them from the one binary.

Any language Zero dependencies Nothing to rebuild
Five agents already ship with her

Including the ones that play music and video from your own folders and make the pet dance along. The guide explains the contract, so writing your own is about thirty lines.
