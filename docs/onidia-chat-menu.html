In the middle: the conversation

1

The message list shows the whole chat

Every reply she writes and every thing you say is listed here, newest at the bottom, oldest at the top. Text, her replies and the pictures she sends all sit next to each other, so you can read back the conversation exactly as it happened.

2

Her picture is the window to her feelings

Each of her replies shows up as a little picture on her side of the window, so you can see whether she is happy, tired or thinking without reading a single word. The look she carries changes with what is happening — a joke gets a smile, a sad moment gets a quieter one.

3

Scroll to read old messages

The list is always available to look back on. Answers to questions you asked hours ago, the joke you made her laugh, the picture you sent: it never disappears unless you delete it.

At the bottom: sending your message

1

Type your message

The text field sits at the bottom. Anything you type here is the message she will read and answer. Keep it normal — she is built for chat, not for commands: say hello, ask a question, tell her about your day.

2

Attach a picture (optional)

The file button next to the text field lets you attach a photo. She will look at it and answer in words about what she sees, the same way she would if you handed her a photo and asked about it.

3

Send with Enter or the button

Press Enter to send, or click the send arrow. Send, and she answers as usual; her reply lands in the list above with her picture on it, and her mouth only moves if voice is set up.

The gear menu

A small gear icon — the last thing you need — opens the settings. Everything in there is a simple choice you make once, not a developer's toolbox, so we run through the main ones below.

The gear menu is where the app keeps the things that do not change during a conversation: who she is, how she sounds, and what she does when she is not talking.

1

CHARACTER — choose who she is

Pick Onidia, or Kama if you have him installed. The window, her replies and her picture all follow your choice, so she becomes him the moment you switch. The change sticks — you do not have to keep choosing it.

2

VOICE — choose how she speaks

Pick the voice that will answer her out loud. If there is none, she answers in text only. A voice setup guide will add one; a free demo voice is included so she can talk from the first day if you want her to.

3

NOTIFICATIONS — choose how she waits for you

Turn them on if you want a small sound or a pop-up when she has answered you, off if you would rather watch the window yourself. The setting decides whether she tells you she is ready, or whether you find out by looking.

4

UNITS — choose how she shows the weather

Temperatures on her face are shown in either Celsius or Fahrenheit depending on what you prefer — a setting for the region you live in. Match the units to your day-to-day weather report and she will match them too.

5

AUCTION — choose when she closes

The window can close after a certain amount of quiet. Pick a time and she will not sit unattended for longer than you like: the longer the silence goes on, the sooner she will go away. This is the "don't leave me alone" switch — keep it off if you want her to stay, whatever happens.

Settings only matter between sessions

If the window is open when you change a setting, nothing happens until you close and reopen it. Changes like her voice, character and notifications are the ones that apply the next time you start the app.
