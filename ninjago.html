Art built to match the real minifigure: red ninja wrap with only the eye band showing (untick ninja hood to see the full face print — stern brows, frown, scar across the eye and chin dimple), crossed brown ropes with a gold medallion and the dark-red undershirt marks on the torso, the dark-red belt with its knot and hanging ends, red arms with black claw hands, red hips and legs. Colours are the official LEGO® ones: Red #C91A09, Dark Red #720E0F, Yellow #F5CD2F. He blinks, walks, waves, spinjitzus, dances, hops and naps; chatter comes from his own phrase pool, each line with the face he makes while saying it. Everything runs in this one file — no build step, no dependencies.
